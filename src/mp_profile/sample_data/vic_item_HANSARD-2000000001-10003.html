Questions without notice and ministers statements

Hospital waiting lists

Pat LEE (Lakeside) (14:05): My question is to the Minister for Health. How many patients are waiting for elective surgery at Lakeside Hospital?

Jordan BLAKE (Hilltop – Minister for Health) (14:06): I thank the member for Lakeside for her question. Elective surgery waiting lists grew during the winter flu season, and the government has funded an additional 400 surgeries at Lakeside Hospital this year.

We are also hiring 500 more nurses across regional hospitals and opening a new mental health hub so patients receive care sooner and closer to home.

Pat LEE: Will the minister release the hospital data?
