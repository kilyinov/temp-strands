Rental Reform Bill 2019

Second reading

Alex MORGAN (Eastvale – Minister for Housing) (10:02): I move:

That this bill be now read a second time.

One in three Victorian households now rent, and many renters live with the constant worry of a no-reason eviction. This bill ends no-reason evictions, caps rent increases to once a year and sets minimum standards so every rental home has working heating and a secure lock.

When I served in the federal Parliament I saw how housing supply shapes every other part of people's lives. Here in Victoria we are building 12,000 new social housing dwellings and reforming planning rules so that more homes can be built near transport.

Pat LEE (Lakeside) (10:20): Landlords in Lakeside have told me rent caps will reduce housing supply.

Members interjecting.

The SPEAKER: Order! The member for Lakeside will be heard in silence.

Alex MORGAN: The member for Lakeside said this morning:

The housing crisis is a myth invented by renters' lobby groups.

I do not accept that. Thousands of families are waiting for social housing, and homelessness services in Eastvale report record demand from renters who have never needed help before.

Motion agreed to.
